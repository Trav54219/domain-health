Domain health

Paste your sending domains. Get MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC and domain age for all of them at once, with the exact record to add when one fails.

What gets checked

MX
The domain can receive replies and bounces, and which provider hosts it.
SPF
Exactly one record, its DNS lookups counted through every include against the limit of 10, a sensible all at the end, and your mail provider included.
DKIM
A public key at 33 common selectors, plus yours. A miss can still mean a custom selector, so it's a warning, not a failure.
DMARC
One record with a policy. Gmail and Yahoo require one from bulk senders.
Domain age
From the registry. Under 30 days old is too new to send cold email.
Website
The domain resolves, so a prospect who types it in lands on something.
Blocklists
Not checked in the browser: Spamhaus and SURBL refuse lookups from public DNS. The command-line version checks them from your own network and can run daily with a Slack alert.

Runs in your browser. Each domain is sent to Cloudflare's and Google's public DNS and to its registry's RDAP server. Nothing is stored.
